Overview

BioGlow Tiles™ are living, self-powered lighting panels. They use engineered bioluminescent organisms (the goal is safe, non-toxic strains of algae and fungi) to give a soft, ambient glow without mains electricity or batteries.

They are designed for everyday consumers as a sustainable, beautiful, cost-saving alternative to outdoor and indoor lighting. They also aim to absorb CO₂ and add to urban greening.

Core Features

  • Zero power required Clarified

    No batteries and no wiring. Photosynthetic strains take their energy from ordinary daylight, as plants do.

  • Natural bioluminescence Plausible

    A soft glow that is gentler on wildlife than many LED lights.

  • Carbon-negative goal Needs proof

    Each tile aims to capture up to 0.5 kg of CO₂ a year during its life. See the evidence ledger for what must be shown first.

  • Long-lasting glow Design target

    Runs for 6–12 months before it needs a bio-refill kit.

  • Customizable designs Planned

    Multiple colors, patterns and brightness levels. Colour packs are on the 2026 roadmap.

How It Works

  1. Bioluminescent microorganisms. Non-toxic algae and fungi live in a nutrient gel.
  2. Transparent bioreactor panel. It protects and sustains the organisms, with air vents for gas exchange.
  3. Self-sustaining cycle. Organisms use captured CO₂ and trace nutrients, topped up by refill kits.
  4. Natural light output. A design target of 20–60 lumens per tile for paths, patios or mood lighting.

Use it: picture the tile as a sealed mini-pond. Air moves through the vents, the gel feeds the glow, and you swap the refill kit when it dims.

Manufacturing & Scalability

Materials

  • Recycled bioplastics for the casing
  • Nutrient gel (renewable and safe)
  • Engineered, long-lived algae and fungal strains

Production

  • Injection-molded and filled in local or microfactories
  • Estimated $5–$10 production cost per unit
  • Retail-ready at $20–$50, with bulk décor packs

Scalability

  • Franchise-ready DAO model so local entrepreneurs can make and sell tiles
  • Shelf-stable before activation and easy to ship globally

A DAO is a community-run organisation whose rules are public. Its governance design is a future extension.

Use Cases

  • Home & garden lighting

    Pathways, patios, fences or interior mood lighting.

  • Off-grid living

    Cabins, RVs and campsites without electricity.

  • Urban infrastructure

    Rooftops, parks, or streetlight alternatives.

  • Event & festival decor

    Ambient, eco-friendly lighting for gatherings.

Safety note: keep conventional lighting wherever safety depends on seeing clearly, such as stairs, roads and exits, until measured brightness is published.

Environmental Impact

  • Zero energy use. The README says each tile replaces about 20 kWh a year of electric lighting.
  • CO₂ absorption. Organisms capture carbon during their life.
  • Wildlife-friendly. Minimal light pollution supports nocturnal species.
  • Fully compostable. End-of-life tiles can be composted or recycled, once safety is verified.

Safety & Stewardship

  • Containment first

    Sealed, tamper-evident units with clear labels: what is inside, and what to do if one cracks. No release into soil or water.

  • Independent review before sale

    Engineered organisms are regulated, for example by the US EPA under TSCA and by EU authorities. Plan biosafety review early. This is not legal advice.

  • Open measurements

    Publish brightness, glow over time, CO₂ and life-cycle data so anyone can check the numbers.

  • Take-back at end of life

    Return and safe-inactivation instructions before any composting claim. A take-back map is a future extension.

  • Invitations, not pressure

    The README calls the glow viral by design. Keep sharing and buying opt-in, with no countdown timers, guilt or hidden defaults.
